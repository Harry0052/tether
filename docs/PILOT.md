# Tether pilot plan

This plan has not been run. There is no evidence in this repository of a real household using Tether. Do not treat the seeded sample family as a pilot result.

## Who

10 to 30 adults who already help a parent, an older relative, or a friend with something small and non-financial. Not a medical decision, not a bank transfer, not a government form. One owner and one helper per family is enough.

## What they do

1. The owner creates one checklist from a template, or writes their own steps.
2. The owner copies the invitation link and sends it themselves. Tether does not email it.
3. The helper accepts, suggests one step, and the owner approves or rejects it.
4. A week later, ask whether they opened the same checklist again.

## What to count

From the database, for those families only:

- Checklists finished after the owner approved a helper. Settings shows this count for the signed-in owner. It is not a site-wide number.
- Invitations that were accepted.
- Helpers whose access was removed.

Ask in conversation, and write it down outside the app:

- Did anyone feel they had given away a password? If yes, stop.
- Did anyone need Tether to submit something on another website? If yes, do not build that. The owner does that on the source site.

## Decision

- Ship a wider trial only if several families finish a checklist with an approval and come back without asking for password sharing.
- Iterate if they finish the checklist but the invitation link or the approval step confuses them.
- Stop if they feel less safe, or if the only way the job gets done is covert access or impersonation.

No compliance claim follows from this plan.
